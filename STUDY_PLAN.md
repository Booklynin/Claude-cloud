## 2. The story (order of topics)

One storyline, five chapters. Each chapter ends by handing a question to the next.

1. **"How did we get here?"** — 100 years of IT in 5 phases → *what is a company actually made of today?*
2. **"What is a company's technology made of?"** — two stacks (ATS vs ETS), the layers, the 2×2 → *what is the biggest piece of the enterprise stack?*
3. **"The backbone"** — ERP and the Megatherm case → *what does it look like when a whole company has to change?*
4. **"A company that really changed"** — DBS: digital transformation, data, AI, culture
5. **"Exam day toolkit"** — model answers, flashcards, mock paper, 1-page cheat sheet

## 3. Phases and how each is taught

| Phase | Topics (in order) | Teaching style |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · The 100-Year Story** | The organising idea (cheap → scarce) · Phase 1 Automate · 2 Inform (shadow IT) · 3 Integrate (ERP + web, Y2K, CIO) · 4 Intelligence (cloud, capex→opex, learning loops) · 5 Autonomy (agentic AI) · who led / who died · India across phases · cost curves, Moore's law, "cheaper never meant less" · Nolan caution | Clickable timeline + flip-cards ("what got cheap / what became scarce") + match-the-phase quiz |
| **2 · The Two Stacks** | "Stack" means two things: ATS vs ETS · interface→infrastructure layers ("problems reported at the top, caused further down") · 6-layer enterprise stack · system of record · integration layer (n(n-1)/2 maths) · standardise vs customise (the 2×2: Unification, Coordination, Diversification, Replication) · configure vs customise · governance/security/compliance · where the money goes (22%, run vs change) · IaaS/PaaS/SaaS | Layered "building" diagram you click through + sort-the-technology game + "locate the layer" puzzles |
| **3 · ERP & Megatherm** | What ERP is (one shared data model) · 4 enterprise-architecture layers · 4 cycles (P2P, Plan-to-Produce, O2C, R2R) · vendors · India SME / GST / e-invoicing · SAP maintenance cliff · Megatherm story · the 5 options · scoring model, weights, TCO · on-prem vs cloud · lesson of the case | Step-by-step process walkthroughs (P2P, O2C) + **interactive decision simulator** (re-weight like Finance vs Top Management and watch the winner change) |
| **4 · DBS** | "Damn Bloody Slow" → why change · 3 pillars · technology overhaul · early AI failures (A*STAR, Watson) · GANDALF · early wins (ATM, IVR, AML, Digibank India) · talent (85% outsourced → insourced) · Data First, ADA, cloud (99% off physical servers) · two-in-a-box · PURE · 1,000 experiments/yr · API platform · new businesses · reskilling · results · what next | Scroll-through journey timeline + "match the initiative to its result" + scenario cards |
| **5 · Exam Toolkit** | Model answers for all 31 short + 8 long questions (with mark structure) · flashcards · timed mock quiz · one-page revision sheet | Flashcards + self-test with instant feedback + printable cheat sheet |

**Look and feel:** light, bright, warm colours only (no dark themes). Every concept gets a real-world hook (a tea-shop chain running the first business computer, Tesco-style loyalty data, an induction-furnace maker, a bank once called "Damn Bloody Slow").

**Delivery format:** one interactive HTML study guide, one tab per phase, mobile-friendly, progress ticks; also saved in this repo so it survives.
